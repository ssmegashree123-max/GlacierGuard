IMPORTANT
- Satellite is a post-model prior in this version, not an XGBoost training feature.
- Historical training data has no real seismic variation, so seismic is wired into the
  feature schema but cannot yet be learned as a predictive signal.
- Water/vibration/tilt/turbidity are simulated in the current prototype feed; weather
  and seismic channels are fetched from external live sources when available.
